A Few Words From My Heart

Dear Athira,

I honestly wasn't sure what to write here, because there are a lot of things I could say, but not everything is easy to put into words..

We've had our good moments, difficult moments, misunderstandings, and times when we were close and times when we were apart. A lot has happened between us, and I know things aren't always simple.

I don't know what the future holds for us, and I don't want to make any promises about things I can't be certain about. But one thing I do know is that you've been a meaningful part of my life, and the time we've shared will always have its own place in my memories. So, on your birthday, I just want to wish you genuinely good things.

I hope this new year of your life brings you happiness, peace, and success, and the kind of moments that make you truly smile. I hope you achieve the things you've been working towards and find more reasons to be happy with yourself and your life.

And even though life doesn't always go the way we expect, I hope you always remember that you are someone who mattered to me, and someone I will always wish the best for.

I made this little birthday page for you because I wanted to do something a little different this year. It's not perfect, but I put some time and effort into making it myself, in my own way.

That's all I wanted to say.

Happy Birthday, my beautiful girl. ❤️

Take care of yourself, keep smiling, and have a beautiful year ahead.

Forever yours,
Abhinav ❤️
